Street Tally

Count what's on your street

Street Tally helps people check the places around them for a local campaign, like which bus stops have a shelter, a bench or light at night. The answers go on OpenStreetMap, the free map anyone can use.

Got a campaign link?

Open it on your iPhone, or scan the code on the flyer with your camera. It takes you straight to the campaign.

Got an email from us?

Open the link in the email on a computer to put your saved answers on the map.

"Not sure" is always a fine answer. You can't break anything.

Street Tally is open source and built on OpenStreetMap.
