Street Tally

Count what's on your street

Street Tally helps people check the places around them for a local campaign, like which entrance doors open by themselves or which bus stops have a shelter. The answers go on OpenStreetMap, the free map anyone can use.

Got a campaign link?

Open it on your phone, or scan the code on the flyer with your camera. It takes you straight to the campaign.
